## Your health information answers to you

Doctors, researchers, apps and other patients sometimes need your health information. With MAIA, they ask *you*: your MAIA answers each request for you, following rules you approved. You start from rules your group suggests, check them with a private AI that works only for you, and nothing is shared until you turn sharing on.

### How does MAIA handle a request?

Every request comes in through the same door and is handled the same way:

1. **Who is asking?** MAIA checks what the requester can prove, for example that they confirmed their email address, or that they belong to your group.
2. **What do they want, and why?** For example, your current medications, for treatment.
3. **What do your rules say?** MAIA shares the privacy-filtered copy if a rule allows it, asks you if no rule decides, and declines if a rule says no. The requester is never told which rule decided.

Your health information is never sent by email. You get an email when something was shared or needs your decision, plus a weekly summary, and a log of every request is saved in your folder.

### Who can ask, and how?

- **Someone you give your link to**, such as a new doctor. They open your personal request page, say who they are and what they need, and confirm their email address.
- **Someone asking everyone in the group**, such as a researcher. The group passes the request to every member's MAIA without being able to read the answers, and each MAIA decides for its own patient. The researcher sees only how many members answered.
- **Another member of your group.** Their MAIA asks yours, marked "from a group member", so your rules can treat members differently from strangers.
- **Someone adding a document**, such as a radiology report. If your rules allow it, the report is saved in your folder the next time you open MAIA. Otherwise MAIA asks you first.

### What do rules look like?

Each rule is one sentence you can read, like the ones below. Try changing one. Your group suggests a starting set, and you confirm or edit each rule before it does anything.

### What do I need to get started?

- **Chrome on a computer.** MAIA keeps your own copy of your records in a folder on your computer, which only Chrome (or Microsoft Edge) can do.
- **A verified email address**, so MAIA can tell you about requests.
- **A passkey**, so you can sign back in when a notification email arrives.
- **A few minutes for your Patient Summary** with your current medications. MAIA helps you write it and saves it in your folder as a PDF.

### Why use a standard?

Requests use an internet standard called GNAP (RFC 9635). A doctor's web page, a hospital's computer system and an AI agent all ask the same way, so your rules apply to all of them equally. Each "yes" is tied to a key that only the requester holds, lasts an hour, and can be taken back.

### Who runs this MAIA?

This MAIA is hosted by the group that runs this page. Whoever pays for hosting controls the servers, so you are trusting this group with your account. Your own copy of everything stays in your folder on your computer.
