## Health AI that answers to you

Join a patient group organized around a condition or cause. Fellow patients, your doctors, and researchers can ask for what you choose to share; your private AI screens everything else — even spam. The group suggests sensible sharing rules and you adopt or change them. Your records and AI always stay with you, never the group.

### Why is MAIA necessary, and how does MAIA address the problem?

- **Matching patients for peer support based on their health records.** Patients can share AI-assisted, de-identified summaries of their history.
- **Providing more complete health-record data to researchers.** Patient-controlled records can include sensitive social determinants, wearables, location, and other factors that patients would not share in a hospital record or health information exchange.
- **Providing unbiased decision support to patients.** AI purchased by the hospital suggests what's best for the hospital. Private AI, especially when open source, has no conflict of interest.
- **Facilitating innovation and reform in health-care delivery.** When health records are under the patient's control, the patient and their doctor can choose services without lock-in to a hospital network.

### What is MAIA?

- A private, AI-enabled patient-controlled health record designed to give patients and doctors fast, convenient access to more complete patient medical records.
- An open-source service incorporating the latest technology to ensure optimum security, transparency, and usability.
- An ongoing project developed by volunteer physicians and medical-technology experts as a public good.
- MAIA has been published in [NEJM AI](https://ai.nejm.org/stoken/default+domain/MJAMGE2HGAZPX58JA8VB/full?redirectUri=doi/full/10.1056/AIp2600289).
- [maia.trustee.ai](http://maia.trustee.ai/) is a non-commercial example, open to all users.

### How is MAIA different from existing health-record systems?

- You control where your records are stored — not a hospital or third-party vendor.
- Your private AI can access the records, and your settings determine what anyone else can access.
- When you join a patient interest group — for hemophilia or cancer, for example — MAIA suggests rules to govern participant access to your records, which you can adopt or modify. Your records and private AI always remain separate from the group.
- Your private AI helps explain and manage your specific access rules.
- Your private AI can charge requesting parties to offset the cost of spam filtering or recoup the cost of hosting your own records.

### Trustee Group Example

- The Trustee group offers peer support for your own MAIA from the developers and other users. Join the Trustee group below whether you have health records or not.
- Some members of the Trustee group have added health records that you can try and access by making a request. Depending on the member's settings you can view a de-identified patient summary or other components and you can send private messages.
- Your request will be sent to every member of the group for their private AI and settings to process.
- If you provide a verified email address, responses will be sent to you as they come in.
- Your request may be ignored as spam, forwarded to the patient as a message, or you might get a suggestion for how to improve your request from the patient's private AI.

### MAIA is the future of health-records management

- MAIA is a public, volunteer-driven initiative that hopes to become sustainable as patients, groups, and physicians grow comfortable with patient-controlled, open-source health records and AI.
- Any individual or group can implement, customize, and extend the MAIA methodology. The open-source code is available in our GitHub repository.

### How much does MAIA cost?

- Any individual or group can open their own hosting account at DigitalOcean, a similar commercial vendor, or even on their own computer.
- DigitalOcean hosting currently costs about $10/month (encrypted group messaging and private AI only) to $50/month (including annotated health records accessible to private AI) for up to a few dozen users.
- At $50/month for 24 users, MAIA hosting costs about **$2 per user per month** — less than a cup of coffee.
- These costs can be offset by access payments for unsolicited requests — for example, from a pharmaceutical company interested in specific data that patients or groups are willing to share.

### MAIA by Trustee Privacy Configuration

- Trustee is the name of the group associated with this example of the MAIA methodology. It is intended to demonstrate privacy features while also driving adoption by patients, physicians, researchers, and group administrators who need to understand that MAIA can be configured very differently to reflect different interests.
- Once the patient has a MAIA they can easily join other groups without needing a different MAIA. 
- Trustee requires a verified email address to notify you of long task completions and incoming messages. If you want to remain anonymous, use Apple Hide My Email or equivalent service. Your email will not be revealed to group participants. Only the group administrator, me, has access to it.
- Messages in the Trustee group are end-to-end encrypted based on a key saved on your computer. Even the administrator cannot see content. Patient Summary, Lists, Stored Chats and your health records are accessible to the MAIA administrator. They are not accessible to administrators of other groups you might join along with Trustee. External access is controlled by your settings with assistance from your private AI.
- There are dozens of other privacy choices that can be made when configuring your MAIA, your participation in groups, a different MAIA hosting service, and various open weight and frontier AIs. Use a frontier AI like Claude to review the MAIA repository for security practices and how to address your specific privacy concerns.
